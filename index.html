Monthly advances / prepaid contributions

The House is a virtual shared wallet, not a third login. Enter each person’s contribution here; the app uses this balance before counting direct payments.

How the House fund works: The month’s expenses use the available House fund first, in date order. If both friends contributed, the used amount is credited to each in proportion to their contribution. Once the fund runs out, the remaining part of each expense is credited to the person selected in “Paid by”. Unused money stays in the House fund and is not treated as an expense.
